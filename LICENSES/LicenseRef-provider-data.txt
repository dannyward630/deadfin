LicenseRef-provider-data

These files hold small excerpts of third-party metadata, or hand-written
fixtures in the shape of a provider's API response. They are test inputs.

Sources:
- internal/anime/testdata/anime-list-master.xml: an excerpt of the
  Anime-Lists mapping, https://github.com/Anime-Lists/anime-lists
- internal/anime/testdata/anime-titles.xml.gz: an excerpt of the AniDB
  title dump, https://anidb.net/ (AniDB terms of use apply)
- internal/providers/*/testdata: responses in the shape of the TMDb, AniList,
  MusicBrainz, TheAudioDB and ListenBrainz APIs. Rights in any real
  metadata in them stay with those services and their contributors.

Rights in this data stay with its providers; the GPL grant for Deadfin
covers Deadfin's own work. To have any of it changed or removed, open an
issue at https://github.com/deadfin-org/deadfin/issues.
