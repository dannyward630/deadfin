# Support

## Questions

Ask in the Q&A category of
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/deadfin-org/deadfin/discussions).
Setup help, "how do I", and ideas you want to talk through go there. Check
the [FAQ](docs/alpha/faq.md) and the [documentation](docs/README.md) first.

## Bugs and client reports

Open an issue with a form:
<https://github.com/deadfin-org/deadfin/issues/new/choose>.

- **Bug report**: something fails or behaves differently from Jellyfin.
  Read [reporting-bugs.md](docs/alpha/reporting-bugs.md) and
  [known-differences.md](docs/alpha/known-differences.md) first.
- **Client compatibility report**: the result of a client test. The steps
  are in [client-matrix.md](docs/alpha/client-matrix.md).
- **Feature request**: something Jellyfin does that Deadfin does not, or a
  new idea.

## Security problems

Report them privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
